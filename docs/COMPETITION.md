## 3. Hard Eligibility, Demo & Submission Guardrails

1. **Do Not Begin Project Work Before Hackathon Start**: Participants must not write project code, build prototypes, or author project assets prior to Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 09:00 AM local time. Pre-event preparation is strictly limited to reading challenge briefs, testing public dataset endpoints, choosing a Local Event or the Universal Event, and organizing research notes.
2. **Mandatory Use of NASA or Space Agency Partner Open Data**: Every eligible submission must meaningfully use open datasets from NASA (such as NASA Earthdata, MODIS, VIIRS, NISAR/SAR, POWER, GIBS, or EONET) or official Space Agency Partners (ESA, JAXA, CSA, ISRO, and peers) and cite every dataset used.
3. **Strict 30-Second Video (`<= 0:30`) or 7-Slide Deck (`<= 7` Slides)**: Exceeding 30 seconds (even `0:31`) or 7 slides (including title and intro slides) disqualifies the team from Global Judging. Target `0:28` to `0:29` for video demos.
4. **External Zero-Auth Hosting Only**: The Project page supports direct upload or paste of `JPG`, `PNG`, and `GIF` images (`<= 10 MB`), but does not host videos or document files. Host videos, slide decks, code repositories, and live web apps publicly with zero logins, permissions, or registrations required, and test every link in a private browser window.
5. **FortiGuard Web Filter Classification**: Submit newly deployed project domains early via `https://www.fortiguard.com/faq/wfratingsubmit` (`Category`: `Web-based Applications` or `Reference`, `Company Name`: `N/A`) so corporate and judge firewalls do not block access to the live demo URL.
6. **English & Under-18 Likeness Rule**: All written Project Tab content and slide decks must be in English (non-English video narration requires English subtitles). Project demos and final apps must contain zero images, video, audio, or likenesses of persons under 18 years old per NASA Space Apps Terms and Conditions.
7. **Early `Submit for Judging` & Unlimited Resave**: Click `Save Progress` frequently (toggling the edit `Lock` if teammates collaborate), complete all required fields, tick `Terms and Conditions` plus `Confirm All Work is Original`, and click `Submit for Judging` early to verify the top confirmation banner. Teams can edit and resubmit until Sunday 11:59 PM local time, and judges evaluate the final saved submission. Submitting on time is also required to unlock the Letter-size (`8.5x11in`) PDF Participant Certificate.
